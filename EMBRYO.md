# Embryo Usage Billing

Embryo Usage Billing is Embryo-Zero's fork of [lago](https://github.com/getlago/lago) (0107237, commit 0107237). It does usage-based billing (compute hours, storage).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
